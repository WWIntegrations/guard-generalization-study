WHAT THIS
CAN'T SAY.

This benign-only corpus cannot establish attack recall, precision, AUROC, attack success rate or binary F1. Those metrics are unavailable, not zero.

Recorded verification replays metrics from saved predictions. It does not repeat model inference, authenticate the author or prove when the run happened.

Group membership can overlap. Timing is local and the model order is fixed. Neither is a universal performance claim.

Public metric replay needs the recorded files. Full LoRA inference also needs the matching adapter weights and runtime.
